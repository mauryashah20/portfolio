About Me

I am an Embedded Systems Developer focusing on drone control networks, low-latency communication links, and edge computing. I specialize in designing and prototyping robust hardware-software systems using the ESP32 platform, custom PCB integrations, and real-time control algorithms.

My work combines hardware engineering with custom protocols (such as ESP-NOW and BLE), computer vision pipelines (using OpenCV and MediaPipe), and autonomous flight systems development.
